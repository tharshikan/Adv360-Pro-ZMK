Adv360 Hyper Layer

Hold A or S and every key on the board fires all four modifiers at once — ⌃⌥⇧⌘ — a namespace no macOS app ships bindings for. The firmware in this repo emits the chords; hammerspoon-tharshi answers them. This page is the map of that contract.

Two repos, one contract

The hyper key doesn't exist on any Mac keyboard, which is the point: the whole ⌃⌥⇧⌘ space is empty, so it can be claimed end-to-end without colliding with anything an app already means. Each side owns half the contract and can change independently — remap a slot without touching firmware, move the layer key without touching Lua.

Every binding, by family

App slots

Toggle behavior: if the slot's app is already frontmost, you bounce back to the previous app. The front four live on the left home row; the right row carries the second tier. ⛶-marked seats (O, E, T, I) open in their own macOS fullscreen space; the rest open as plain windows on the desktop you're already on. ⇣-marked seats (A, H) also sweep the desk as they open — every other window on the current desktop minimizes — and toggling back (or hyper+R) restores what was swept. CP mode and the layout snapshots pull a fullscreen window back to windowed before tiling it.

App-scoped remaps

Not hyper chords — plain shortcuts that mean something different while one app is frontmost, and nothing unusual anywhere else. An application watcher enables them with focus and disables them on the way out; no event taps, so nothing runs per-keystroke. One rule keeps them safe: remaps are one-way — never onto a chord that is itself remapped, or the synthesized keystroke retriggers the hotkey in a loop.

Getting in

  • Hold A (left pinky) or S (right pinky) on any of the three base layers — urob, tharshi, and sunaku all route their pinky home key to layer 14. Tap still types the letter; the hold-tap flavors and timings differ per base but the destination is the same.
  • Two doors on purpose. The layer key can't chord with itself: reaching hyper+A (Ghostty) means holding S, and hyper+S (Slack) means holding A. Every other key is reachable from either door.
  • One-handed shortcut: the num layer (hold N) plants hyper D H T N S under the right home row and hyper B above it — Finder, WebStorm, Notes, Slack, the fullscreen toggle, and the clock card without the left hand.

Firmware notes

  • Hyper is layer index 14. The chords are written out inline as LC(LS(LA(LG(K)))) rather than behind a macro so the Keymap Editor can still parse the bindings (see the comment atop adv360.keymap).
  • The layer covers the full board — letters, numbers, punctuation, arrows, and Enter all emit their hyper chord, so the Mac side can bind new chords without any firmware change.

Hyper is not Meh

The ecosystem splits its two synthetic namespaces by one modifier, and the split is a rule, not a habit:

⌃⌥⇧⌘
hyper · this page

Talks to the desk: apps, windows, layouts, system toggles. Entered by holding A / S. Every chord is a Hammerspoon hotkey in init.lua's app/window ecosystem.

⌃⌥⇧
meh · text nav 2

Talks to the text: vim-grade intents (diw, dd, visual mode) sent from the Text Nav 2 layer (hold O) and translated per-app by the same init.lua. Documented in the hammerspoon-tharshi README.

⌘ present → you're talking to the desk.
⌘ absent → you're talking to the text.

Meh+arrows are deliberately bound nowhere on the Mac side — ⌃⌥⇧-arrows belong to the JetBrains IDEs and pass through untouched. The hyper layer never appears on Text Nav 2, and no Meh chord appears here.

Text Nav 2, drawn

The Meh side's board: hold O (left ring) on the sunaku base and layer 15 comes up — the right hand alone moves and deletes while the left just holds. Teal cells send Meh intents that hammerspoon-tharshi interprets per-app; plain cells are native macOS chords or firmware macros, so the whole delete row works with Hammerspoon off. Blank keys fall through and simply type.
